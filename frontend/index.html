NASA Earth System Trend Detective Tour

Welcome, Detective! Follow the 4 core investigative clues to examine how multi-decadal climate signals are rigorously verified from NASA satellite observations.

CLUE 1

Empirical Monotonic Drift

Ordinary linear regressions (OLS) assume normally distributed noise and constant variance. EarthSift deploys Sen's median-of-slopes and the non-parametric Mann-Kendall test to discover real physical signals immune to extreme sensor outliers.

CLUE 2

The Red Noise Trap

Climate inertia creates serial persistence that fools standard trend tests into declaring false positives. EarthSift applies Hamed & Rao (1998) variance correction on detrended residuals to verify true physical trends.

CLUE 3

Spatial Regimes (Anselin LISA)

Isolated pixel anomalies could be noise. Using Local Indicators of Spatial Association (LISA) and Cliff & Ord Moran's I, EarthSift isolates contiguous High-High (Hotspots) and Low-Low (Refugia) spatial clusters.

CLUE 4

Dual Compound Hazards (T + P)

Heatwaves alone are dangerous; heat combined with precipitation decline creates catastrophic aridification. EarthSift's Compound Aridification Index (CAI) cross-analyzes multivariate divergence.
